# Offers lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Real pain, stated unprompted.** A kirana owner asked for this ("so many people call to order; keeping track of it all is hard"). The buyer already tracks udhaar in a paper diary, so the problem is lived, not invented. That is one voice, not proof, but it points at the right pain.
- **Low effort asked of the buyer.** No new number, no new app, no typing. The owner keeps talking the way they already talk and confirms with one tap. On the value equation, effort and sacrifice is the strongest of the four, and it is the exact weakness of the ledger apps.
- **Fast time to first value, in principle.** The capture loop is live: message to structured order to confirm card to logged. The owner sees their own orders organised on day one, with no setup project.
- **A stackable offer.** Order capture, khata, derived stock and ask-in-Hindi are separate problems on the buyer's path. Several are cheap to deliver once orders are captured.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No proof, so likelihood is near zero.** There are no active or paying shops, and the DB was wiped. The headline outcome (ask your business anything) is not built. Retention is the stated goal, yet there is no retention data. Half the orders come by phone call, and call ingestion is not built, so the product today covers only the WhatsApp half. Until a real shop runs it for weeks, the offer is a promise.
2. **The market is unscored, and the money does not come from the buyer.** The offer is free to the shopkeeper. Revenue depends on fees from udhaar recovery, NBFC lending spread and network take-rate, none of which exist. Whether these buyers will pay, or whether lenders will underwrite on this order history, is untested. Market size, price and revenue per shop are all missing. A free product for a low-margin buyer is a weak market until proven otherwise.
3. **Perceived effort is higher than the pitch says.** The owner still has to move customers and vendors onto WhatsApp, confirm every order, and trust the books. Dev-mode limits (24-hour window, no Live-mode button taps, template approval for proactive messages) mean the experience on a real phone is not yet the experience promised. An undifferentiated "AI assistant for shops" is also easy to copy on the surface, and the offer has no guarantee or named outcome to separate it.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Name five shops by name who will run this daily for 30 days. What happens in week three, when the novelty fades?
- What is the one outcome the owner feels first (fewer missed orders, money collected, time saved), and how do you measure it in the first week?
- What do you do about phone calls, which are half the orders, before call ingestion exists?
- Who pays, how much, and when? Would the owner pay a fixed fee for this, or only a share of collections? (open: price and revenue per shop; /founder-cfo)
- How many target shops exist and how many can you reach per cluster? (open: market size; /founder-consumer)
- What does a Khatabook or OkCredit user lose by switching, and what stops them adding your layer on top? (/founder-competitors)
- What is the guarantee? What do you promise, and what do you refund or do if it does not work?
- What does acquiring and serving one shop cost, including AI/API cost per month? (open: CAC and cost to serve; /founder-cfo)

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- A pilot of a small set of real shops live on WhatsApp in Live mode (Meta Business Verification done), with usage and retention measured over at least several weeks, and a stated threshold agreed up front.
- The ask-in-Hindi feature working on real captured data in those shops, since it is the headline promise.
- Evidence that owners confirm orders and come back unprompted, and a plan for the phone-call half of orders.
- One tested money path (most plausibly udhaar collections) with a real price or fee, and an early read on cost to serve per shop.
- A named outcome and a guarantee the offer can stand behind, and market sizing from /founder-consumer and /founder-competitors before the full seed is released.
