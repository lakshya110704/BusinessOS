# BusinessOS

- What it is: A WhatsApp-native AI operating system for Indian small shopkeepers. It captures every order — raw material from vendors and goods to customers, by text or Hindi voice note — with zero typing, builds the shop's books (khata + stock) automatically, and lets the owner ask their business anything in plain Hindi.
- Who it is for: Indian SMB owners, starting with kirana (neighbourhood grocery) and similar small shops. They take dozens of orders a day across phone calls and WhatsApp, keep udhaar (informal credit) in a paper diary, hold stock in their head, and have never used an ERP or CRM. The owner *is* the system today.
- What it sells, at what price: The operations layer (order capture, the "ask your business" assistant, the auto-khata) is given away free to drive adoption. Money is made on the financial flow it sits on: a fee on udhaar recovered (collections), an interest spread on working-capital lending via an NBFC partner (credit underwritten on real order history), and a transaction take-rate on the verified buyer-supplier network. No per-seat SaaS fee — the stated belief is this merchant won't pay for tidiness, only for money recovered.
- Where and how: India. Built directly on the Meta WhatsApp Cloud API (no app to install, no new number). AI is GPT-4o mini (intent + entities) and OpenAI Whisper (Hindi/Hinglish voice). Go-to-market is in-person, winning one dense geographic cluster of shops at a time.
- Budget and constraints: Raising a $2M seed for 18 months. The capture loop (message -> understand -> confirm on WhatsApp -> order logged to khata) is already live end-to-end on real infrastructure (Meta, GPT-4o + Whisper, Supabase, Upstash). The conversational "ask your business" assistant, call-recording ingestion, and derived stock/dashboard are not yet built. Known blocker: Meta Business Verification is required to flip from dev mode to Live mode before real inbound webhooks (and proactive template messages) work on a real phone at scale.
