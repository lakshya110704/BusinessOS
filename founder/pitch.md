BusinessOS, a WhatsApp assistant for your shop. First 3 months free, then 500 rupees a month.
Forward or speak your orders in Hindi on WhatsApp, text or voice note, and it reads each one and sends you a card to confirm with one tap. No new app or number.
It builds each customer's udhaar khata and keeps your stock for you, and you can ask it in Hindi who owes you and what to reorder.
It can also send polite reminders for unpaid udhaar when you tell it to. Phone-call orders can be added by recording the call.
Set up by a person visiting your shop.
