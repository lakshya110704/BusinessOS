A live cost model. The question the council said nobody answered: at ₹500–1,000/month, does serving one trader actually turn a profit — on WhatsApp alone, and once you add call capture?

Defaults are grounded estimates (GPT-4o-mini $0.15/$0.60 per 1M tok · Whisper $0.006/min · WhatsApp utility template ~₹0.12 · telephony ~₹0.60/min · USD→₹88). Every number is editable — change any assumption and everything recomputes. CAC (cost to acquire a trader) is deliberately excluded — this models cost-to-serve only; see the note at the bottom.

How to read this
  • WhatsApp-only cost-to-serve is tiny (mostly a rupee or two of OpenAI) — so the margin per merchant is almost all ARPU. The real barrier to profit here is fixed infra at low scale and, far bigger, CAC (below), not compute.
  • Call capture is the economics bomb. Telephony minutes + per-minute transcription scale with how much they talk — and these traders talk a lot. Watch the margin flip red and the "min ARPU to break even" jump. If it exceeds what a trader will pay, call capture needs cheaper transcription (self-hosted Whisper), fewer routed minutes (only key calls), or a much higher price.
  • CAC is not in here. Selling in-person to price-sensitive, anti-SaaS traders could cost ₹1,000s per signup. A merchant contributing ₹385/mo takes months just to repay acquisition — model that separately before trusting any break-even number above.
